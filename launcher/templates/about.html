I'm Professor Paul Fergus, and I'm thrilled to be teaching on this innovative postgraduate program. With over two decades of experience in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics, I bring a blend of academic insight and industry expertise to the course. I serve as an NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute (DLI) Instructor and University Ambassador, helping to advance AI education globally. I am also the co-founder of Trap Tracker, where we leverage AI for environmental conservation and wildlife monitoring.

Together with Professor Carl Chalmers — Research Scientist in AI Sensing and Inference Systems at Microsoft — I co-authored the book Applied Deep Learning: Tools, Techniques, and Implementation, which serves as a core resource for this MSc programme. It offers an in-depth guide to the tools, techniques, and practical implementations of deep learning, designed to give students both theoretical understanding and hands-on experience.

This programme is designed to immerse you in the latest developments in AI, with a focus on machine-learning techniques that are shaping industries worldwide. You'll gain the practical experience needed to address real-world challenges — preparing you for roles in research, industry, or entrepreneurship.

Throughout the module I look forward to sharing my experience and guiding you through this transformative field. Together we'll explore how AI and machine learning can drive innovation and impact across sectors.

Feel free to connect on LinkedIn, browse my research contributions on Google Scholar, or have a look at our wildlife-monitoring work at Trap Tracker.

Welcome to the journey.

Paul
