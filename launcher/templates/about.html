I'm Professor Paul Fergus. With over two decades of experience in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data analytics, I bring a unique blend of academic insight and industry expertise. In addition to my role as a professor, I serve as an NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute (DLI) Instructor and University Ambassador, helping to advance AI education globally. I am also the Co-Founder of Conservation AI, which uses computer vision to detect poaching threats and monitor endangered species from camera-trap footage — deployed with partners including Chester Zoo, Knowsley Safari, and the Endangered Wildlife Trust, with our research published in Nature — and the Founder of Trap Tracker, where we build AI tools for wildlife monitoring and conservation.

Together with Professor Carl Chalmers — Research Scientist in AI Sensing and Inference Systems at Microsoft — I co-authored the book Applied Deep Learning: Tools, Techniques, and Implementation, an in-depth guide to the tools, techniques, and practical implementations of deep learning. My latest book, Optimised: The Silent Surrender to Intelligent Machines, is due for publication later this year.

Feel free to connect on LinkedIn, browse my research contributions on Google Scholar, or have a look at our wildlife-monitoring work at Trap Tracker.
